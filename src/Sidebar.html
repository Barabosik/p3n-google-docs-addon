API key

Without a key, p3n gives you 10 calls an hour, up to 5,000 characters each. For more, sign up (you get free credit), create a key on the API keys page and paste it here.

Saved as soon as p3n accepts it.

What p3n sends

Only the text you choose, and only when you click Check or Rewrite. It goes from your browser to api.p3n.si and nowhere else. p3n reads your selection only when you click, keeps no history and has no analytics.

Your key is kept in your Google account's settings for this add-on, never in the document, so other people with access to the document can't see it and it doesn't travel with a copy.
