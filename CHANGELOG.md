## 0.1.4

Committed, not deployed: it goes to Google as a new script version only after OAuth verification passes. The scopes are unchanged.

- The sidebar's footer links to p3n.si (**p3n.si ↗**, after the free calls or balance line, with or without a key). It opens in your browser.
- After Check, the sentences the result lists are highlighted in the document, and the pattern hits in a lighter colour. They come off on the next Check or Rewrite, or with Clear highlights under the result. The highlights are a background colour written into the document, so the sidebar says that anyone who can open it sees them; clearing puts back each piece of text's own background.
